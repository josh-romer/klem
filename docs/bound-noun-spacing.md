For twelve reviewed KRDict bound-noun identities, `spacing.modifier_bound_noun`
separates an independently recovered modifier or nominalizer from the noun.
For example, `먹어준것은` offers `먹어준 것은` and `먹어 준 것은`;
`공부하기때문에` offers `공부하기 때문에`. Auxiliary witnesses stop before
the noun, and the viewer retains each word's readings and optional joined
relationship. The supported nouns are 것, 수, 줄, 바, 적, 지, 듯, 뿐, 대로,
만큼, 때문 and 따름, with source-specific modifier classes. These options
share the existing work/output bounds and preserve earlier options. Possessive
or bare nominal modifiers, additional noun identities and contextual sense
selection remain outside this reviewed template.
