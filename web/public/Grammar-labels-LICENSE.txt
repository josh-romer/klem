Grammar teaching labels in web/src/grammar-labels.json

Adapted from the Korean Basic Dictionary (한국어기초사전), National Institute
of Korean Language (국립국어원). Based on the September 2026 dictionary export.
Changes: English descriptions condensed into short labels for common functions.
These labels do not enumerate all senses or assert a contextual interpretation.
Source entry numbers, original headwords and POS values appear alongside each
label in the JSON catalog. The browser hover text lists every label source.
The same catalog supplies reviewed expression/component lookups in klem-web.
An individual source entry is at:
https://krdict.korean.go.kr/kor/dicSearch/SearchView?ParaWordNo=ENTRY_NUMBER

The catalog includes the additional senses informing combined labels. Bundled
forms and contracted/spaced expression mappings have explicit explanatory notes.

Labels may also have explicit primary references where the pinned dictionary
has no entry. These are agent-authored summaries of grammatical function,
licensed as part of this label catalog; references do not create dictionary
entries. For the literary/formal (으)리다 final bundle, see:
임동훈 (National Institute of Korean Language), 어미의 사전적 처리 (1998), §2.2

The browser lists the reference title and provides a separate source link.
